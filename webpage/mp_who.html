Cyberstorm may be played with up to eight people on a local area network or head-to-head via modem. Requirements for network play are an IPX network with all players on the same network hub or node.

Requirements for modem play are a 14.4K baud modem or better, or a “null” modem connection. Two players with varying modem speeds will not adversely affect the play balance.

Network play may be conducted in one of three ways:

- Teams of players against each other (with or without Cybrids as an added element).

- Free-for-all with each player fighting individually until the last Herc is left standing (again, with or without Cybrids as an added element).

- Humans vs. Cybrids with the Cybrid Force Level cranked up all the way to emulate an Elite Military Mission.

Modem play may be head-to-head only, though Cybrids may be present if desired.
