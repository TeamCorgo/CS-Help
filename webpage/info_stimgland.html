StimGland increases a Bioderm’s percentage to successfully hit with various weapons. There are two varieties of StimGland, Jackup and Placidex. Jackup increases a pilot’s skills by as much as 6% (though this temporary bonus wears off at a rate of 2% per turn), but it also lowers Genetic Stability by 5%, which remains permanent unless Placidex is used, or until healed by a Command Bioderm, or until the Bioderm is healed at the Medvat. Advanced on-board Life Support systems (available at higher ranks) will heal the Bioderm eventually, but very slowly. Jackup also raises a derm’s Toxin Level by up to 10%. Toxicity can be removed during Detox at the Medvat or by an advanced life support system during battle.

Placidex is used to counter Stability loss from Jackup. However, Placidex also raises Toxin buildup by as much as 10%.

All Bioderms have Toxin and Genetic Stability thresholds. When the Toxin level reaches threshold, the Bioderm no longer gains any positive effects from additional injections of StimGland (either Jackup or Placidex). When the Bioderm’s stability is witin 50% of threshold, you will see it in the beginning stages of genetic breakdown, as evidenced by its veiny blue appearance. Once Genetic Stability drops below threshold, the Bioderm’s health decreases every turn. This continues until death (at 0%) or until its Genetic Stability is bolstered by a Command Bioderm.

Between 50% of threshold and threshold, the ’derm loses skill rating at a low rate. Once the instability threshold is reached, the ‘derm loses skill at a much higher rate. If a skill reaches zero, attempting to use that skill will gain the response “Bioderm unable to comply.”

Health and Stability will decrease with every turn if the Toxin level has been exceeded. Also, the Bioderm will not receive any of the positive effects of additional Jackup or Placidex if the Toxin level has been exceeded.

StimGland is administered in the Pilot Status area during battle.
