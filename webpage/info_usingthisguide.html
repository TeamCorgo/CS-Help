Open and Close.

The CyberStorm Herc Commander’s Guide is a complete on-line manual. It’s fast and easy to use. To open it, press the F1 key or use the Help pull-down menu. To close it, click its window close box, or open its File menu and select Exit. You may also refer to your Quick Reference Card during battle.

Contents.

The initial Contents window outlines the major categories and topics. To display a topic page, just click it. From elsewhere in the manual, you can return to this window by clicking the Help Topics button.

Index.

This lets you type in a topic name or choose it from a predefined list.

Find.

This function searches every word in the manual for the one you want.

Topics.

The first topic in the manual is called Table of Contents. This screen offers another way to select help categories and topics. Simply click on a menu item to go there.

Any yellow underlined text you see in a topic is a hypertext “jump” to a related item: simply click on the highlighted text to go there. To return along the path of your jump(s), click the Back button.

To “scroll” down through long articles, click on the scroll bar or the Up/Down arrows at right, or use your [Pg Up] and [Pg Dn] keys.

To page back and forth through topics in sequence, click on the browse buttons [<<] and [>>].

Menu Bar Features.

You can add your own notes in the on-line manual with the Edit menu’s Annotate feature, or save your place anywhere in the manual by defining a Bookmark there. To see a list of all the topics you have been to, select the Display History Window on the Options menu. The History list lets you go directly to any previous topic by double-clicking it.
