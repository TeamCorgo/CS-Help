After you click on Launch Mission at the Herc Base you will be presented with a series of dialog boxes beginning with a name entry screen. This is followed by a Choose Connection Mechanism screen where you make a choice depending on whether you want to enter a network game or a modem game.

Network Play

If you choose Winsock IPX Connection for Directplay, you will be prompted to Create or Connect to a game. If you choose Create, you will be taken to the Launch Multi-Player Menu where you will have control of the game’s make-up. After all decisions are made in that menu and all other players are ready, click Begin Game.

If you are not the game creator, simply choose Connect and the appropriate game in progress, then you will be taken to the Launch Multi-Player Menu (although you will not have control of the game’s make-up). You will only be able to send and receive messages, and set your group color code.

Modem Play

If you choose modem play and you are the player initiating the game, choose Create to get to <Launch Multi-Player Menu From there, make all of the necessary game settings, then when the other party dials in, and clicks their “ready” check box, click Begin Game.

If you are not the game creator, choose modem play, then Connect and Dial your opponent’s modem number. When the connection is made, you will be taken into the Launch Multi-Player Menu (although you will not have control of the game’s make-up). You will be able to send and receive messages, and set your group color code. Check your “ready” box to signal the game creator to begin the game.
