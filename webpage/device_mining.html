All Mining Devices are gradually available to all Hercs.

Mining devices are one way to extract ore while out on missions. Corporate Automining will kick in if you manage to destroy all of the Cybrids in a given mission first. This will mine any remaining ore on the planet.

Activate mining devices from the Internals control panel while in the battlefield. Turning them on will consume some reactor power, but not nearly as much as the actual act of mining will.

Bertrand Ore Extractor – cheap and relatively efficient. workhorseThe most common model used.

Low Energy Extractor - used primarily in high risk areas to allow more power for shields and movement.

Fueller Analyzer Extractor – used near the core to maximize productivity in low-ore areas.

Maxim Extractor – increased carrying capacity for high-ore planets.

Improved Extractor – utilizes the latest technology to merge many of the best qualities of older Extractor models.
